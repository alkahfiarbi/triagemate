# Privacy Policy — TriageMate

Last updated: 18 November 2026

TriageMate is a Forge app for Jira that triages new issues. It is built by Arbi Al kahfi as an entry for Codegeist 2026.

## What data the app accesses

When you ask TriageMate to triage a Jira issue, the app reads that issue's summary, description, priority and labels through the Jira API. It runs with your own Jira permissions, so it can only see issues you can already see.

## What data the app stores

TriageMate does **not** store personal data. It processes the issue text in real time to produce a triage suggestion (type, priority, labels and a short summary) and does not retain the issue content, your name, your email address, or any other personal data after the request finishes. It does not send data to any third party, and it does not use analytics or tracking.
