# Terms of Service — TriageMate

Last updated: 18 November 2026

TriageMate is a Forge app for Jira built by Arbi Al kahfi as an entry for Codegeist 2026. It triages Jira issues by classifying them, suggesting a priority and labels, and drafting a short summary.

## Use

TriageMate's suggestions are generated by a rule-based engine and by the Rovo agent. They are suggestions only. Please review the suggested type, priority and labels before applying them. TriageMate only changes an issue after you confirm.
